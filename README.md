#  Al Khawaja Delivery Management System

> A modern delivery management system built with Laravel for managing orders, customers, drivers, employees, and delivery operations.

## 📌 Overview

Al Khawaja Delivery Management System is a web-based platform designed to organize and simplify the daily operations of a delivery company.

The system provides tools for managing orders, customers, drivers, employees, financial records, notifications, and activity logs through a centralized management system.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📦 Order Management
- Create and manage delivery orders
- Track order status
- Manage customer information
- Search customers by phone number
- Maintain order status history
- Assign orders to drivers

### 👥 Customer Management
- Customer profiles
- Phone-based customer lookup
- Customer order history
- Centralized customer information

### 🛵 Driver Management
- Driver profiles
- Driver accounts
- Vehicle information
- License information
- Vehicle plate management
- Driver status management
- Driver work sessions

### 👨‍💼 User & Employee Management
- User accounts
- Role-based access
- Employee management
- Driver accounts linked to driver profiles
- Account management

### 💰 Financial Management
- Payments
- Expenses
- Financial records

### 🔔 Notifications & Activity
- Notifications
- Activity logging
- Order status history
- Operational tracking
